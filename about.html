Methodology

Flexible Mindset

Different teams, situations, and products call for different development methodologies. Both my role & my tool set are flexible; however, one goal of my involvement is always to facilitate the end goal of the team. My experience spans a variety of development environments including: internal products, external software applications, updates to existing systems and the creation of new systems, all-encompassing software of a massive scope, and the creation of small, single-instance applications. In every instance, the most valuable assets I provided were my unparalleled ability to stay flexible to accomplish the task at hand, and my foresight to see the larger picture.

Creative Problem Solving

My passion for solving puzzles is why I love application development. The heart of system design is creating solutions that will work within constraints. Providing solutions, mitigating project hurdles, and team collaboration is why I love my job.

Aggressive Learner

I love learning; in another life I was a sponge. In today’s world of ever-expanding technology, this is an important asset. There is always more to learn, so pursuing information is vital. My varied work experiences and background prove my belief in the power of acquiring knowledge.

Understanding First

My experience in UX consulting has taught me that successful software design depends on asking the right questions. Challenging assumptions, a thorough understanding of use cases and in-depth research create a transparent decision-making process, which I feel is the backbone for building a good team and a great project.
